Reviewed on 4 October 2026 against the public Google Drive archive linked by the portfolio. All 23 PDFs were downloaded to a temporary directory and inspected through text extraction or embedded certificate images. Original files and Drive permissions were preserved.

- `RPC Certificate.pdf` is participation in Rakuten Product Conference 2026 on 22 April 2026.
- The 5 KM marathon record is participation, not a professional certification.
- The Machine Learning specialization and its three constituent course certificates are distinct documents. All four are retained and labeled accordingly; their count should not be interpreted as four independent specializations.

- Infosys course completion dates differ from their November 2025 issue dates; displayed dates use completion dates.
- No identical PDF duplicates were identified. Similar AI course titles belong to distinct course records and were retained.
